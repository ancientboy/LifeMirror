# Liuyao Knowledge Pack Specification

**Life Mirror Institute · Knowledge Architecture Paper**

---

## Abstract

六爻是传统象征体系的重要组成部分，通过卦象、爻位和变化关系提供观察问题的语言。

Life Mirror 不将六爻作为确定性预测系统，而将其定义为：

> Symbolic Reflection Framework

一种帮助用户探索自身状态、决策和成长方向的象征性反思框架。

---

传统六爻：


Calculation tables and executable user-specific rules do not live in the Knowledge Pack. They belong to the single Liuyao Tool. KNOWLEDGE-003 explains what a computed field means and supplies classical witnesses; it never calculates a user's reading.

## Three Coins Method

三枚铜钱起卦。

用户通过六次摇卦生成完整六爻结构。

```text
第一次 → 初爻
第二次 → 二爻

第四次 → 四爻
```

# 6. Reflection Mapping Layer

Life Mirror 增加现代反思层。

传统解释回答：

> 这个卦象代表什么。

Reflection Mapping 回答：

> 这个象征可以帮助用户思考什么。

例如：

传统：

未济代表尚未完成、处于变化阶段。

Reflection：

- 哪些准备还没有完成？
- 你正在结束什么阶段？
- 你真正想改变的是什么？

# 11. Runtime Knowledge Contract

Version 1.2 makes the separation executable rather than conceptual.

## Classical Knowledge

The versioned pack contains:

- 64 King Wen hexagrams
- 64 hexagram judgments (卦辞)
- 64 Great Image texts (大象)
- 386 line texts, including 用九 and 用六
- 386 Small Image texts (小象)
- deterministic moving-line reading rules

These fields are retrieved from KNOWLEDGE-003 and are never authored by the LLM.

## Symbolic and Reflection Mapping

Modern symbolic summaries, keywords and reflection prompts are stored under separate `symbolic` and `reflectionMapping` objects. They may guide reflection, but they must never be presented as classical quotations.

```text
classical.judgment / classical.image / classical.lines
                            ≠
symbolic.meaning / reflectionMapping.prompt
```

## Retrieval Output

Each calculation retrieves only the matching original and changed hexagrams, exact moving-line texts, rule-meaning records and one deterministic text-selection `readingRule`. The selection rule determines which classical text receives attention; executable 装卦/断卦 rules and their user-specific results stay in TOOL-001.

## Tool-to-Knowledge Mapping

| Tool field | KNOWLEDGE-003 supplies | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `divination.originalHexagram` / `changedHexagram` | 卦名、卦辞、大象、爻辞、小象 | 重算卦象 |
| `structure.palace` / `shi` / `ying` | 卦宫、世应概念说明 | 改写排盘位置 |
| `lines[].relation` / `element` / `spirit` / `void` | 六亲、五行、六神、旬空的规则含义 | 判断某用户的六亲或旬空 |
| `analysis.usefulGod` / `strength` / `relationships` | 用神、旺衰、生克的解释与适用边界 | 另取用神或重判旺衰 |
| `analysis.hiddenSpirit` / `timing` | 伏神飞神、应期的传统含义与低置信提示 | 承诺事件或时间 |
| `evidence[]` | 对应规则出处/释义 | 推翻工具证据 |

The corpus is normalized from public-domain Zhouyi and Image-text witnesses. Automated tests verify every expected judgment, Image and line record before release.

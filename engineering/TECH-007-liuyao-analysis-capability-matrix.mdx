# TECH-007 Liuyao Analysis Capability Matrix

## Purpose

This document is the Phase 2.5 acceptance boundary for the Liuyao interpretation upgrade. The deterministic TypeScript tool is authoritative at runtime; the historical `tools/liuyao/analysis.py` implementation is the migration reference. Shiguang may explain computed evidence but must never calculate or invent a rule.

Health and investment always retain real-world safety limitations. Health uses Shi for the person and separately observes officials as illness pressure and offspring as relief; this intentionally deepens the Python prototype's single `offspring` target.

## Judgment Quality Contract

- Every verdict is scoped to one `intentId`; its decisive evidence IDs must share that scope.
- Key evidence reserves space for every intent before filling remaining slots by weight.
- Support and obstruction are accumulated separately. Material opposition produces `evidenceBalance: conflicted`, a mixed direction and lower confidence.
- Hidden useful gods, weak evidence and high analysis uncertainty reduce confidence.
- Evidence sorting has deterministic tie-breakers. Identical tosses and context must produce deep-equal results.
- Shiguang must use the exact scenario label, state both sides of a conflict, and cannot transfer evidence between intents.

## Deliberate Boundaries

- The LLM does not calculate Liuyao or add terminology absent from Tool output.
- Structural markers never decide success or failure by themselves.
- Timing is a low-confidence observation window, not an event date.
- Classical cold rules not represented by deterministic inputs and regression fixtures are outside Phase 2.5.
- Medical, legal and investment outputs remain bounded by professional verification and real-world evidence.

## Acceptance Tests

The regression suite covers historical Python fixtures, all 64 structures, five Six-Relation-on-Shi states, multiple useful gods, hidden/flying spirit combinations, day/month state combinations, transformations, scenario focus, multi-intent isolation, conflict calibration and repeated-input determinism. Reflection tests enforce answer-first output, separate intent answers, faithful evidence cards, optional reflection and risk-specific limitations.
